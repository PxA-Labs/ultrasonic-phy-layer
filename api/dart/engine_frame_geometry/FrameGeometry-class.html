txFrameSampleBudget({int minPayload = 1}) → int
Sample budget for one transmitted frame that must carry at least minPayload bytes: targetFrameAirtimeSeconds, stretched to twice the smallest such frame when that alone takes more than half of it, and never above SoundwaveNative.maxFrameSamples. The stretch matters for slow CSS settings (SF10 / 2 kHz: a 12-byte link frame already takes ~25 s, mostly preamble and FEC): frames there stay at most twice the minimum, so at least half of their airtime carries payload.

targetFrameAirtimeSeconds → const double
Airtime the TX path aims for per frame. Long frames are fragile (one noise burst or a moved phone loses the whole frame), stall ARQ (each attempt waits a frame's airtime) and give no progress feedback. 15 s keeps OFDM frames full (219-byte payloads take ~1 s) and only splits slow CSS settings (a full frame is ~34 s at SF8 and ~110 s at SF10, 2 kHz; the latter does not even fit SoundwaveNative.maxFrameSamples).
