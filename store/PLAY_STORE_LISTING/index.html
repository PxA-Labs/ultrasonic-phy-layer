    Send text and small files between nearby devices using high-frequency sound.

    (76 characters.)

    Full description (≤ 4000 characters)¶

    Soundwave sends data through the air as sound. Type a message or pick a small file, and Soundwave plays it as a high-frequency signal through your speaker. A nearby phone or computer running Soundwave picks it up with its microphone and decodes it. No internet, Bluetooth, pairing or account is needed.

    Two modulation modes • CSS (chirp spread spectrum): slow but robust. Each frequency sweep carries 6–10 bits, in the 18–20 kHz range with the default settings. • OFDM: much faster, using many subcarriers at once. In the current version its signal is clearly audible. • Presets for a robust long-range setup and a high-speed OFDM setup, plus full manual control of frequency, sweep, spreading factor, FFT size, cyclic prefix and equalizer.

    Built-in error protection Every frame carries a CRC-32 checksum and optional Reed-Solomon error correction. Longer messages and files are split into fragments and reassembled, with optional automatic retransmission (ARQ) when both devices enable it.

    See the signal Live spectrum analyzer, waterfall spectrogram, waveform view and OFDM constellation diagram, with an estimated SNR for every received message.

    Record, import and export Record what the microphone hears as WAV, import WAV/PCM files and decode them offline, save signal samples, and export your message log as JSON, CSV or TXT.

    Private by design No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and the app does not connect to the internet. Audio is processed on your device and nothing leaves it unless you share it. Note that transmissions are broadcast as sound and are not encrypted: anyone nearby with a compatible receiver can hear them, so do not send sensitive information.

    Good to know • Range and speed depend heavily on your speaker, microphone, volume and room. Start with the devices close together. • Most adults hear little of the CSS band, but children and pets may. • Soundwave is experimental, open-source software (Apache 2.0): github.com/PxA-Labs/ultrasonic-phy-layer
